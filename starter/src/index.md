# Preface

This is where the book says who it is for, what it assumes, and how it wants to be read. Three or four sentences do the job on the start page; anything longer belongs in a first chapter. The reader arriving here is deciding whether to continue, so tell them what they will be able to do by the end and what they need before they begin.

Replace this file when the book has something to say. Until then it is a stub, and so is the one demonstration chapter that follows it.
